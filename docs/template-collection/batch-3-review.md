# Batch 3 review

The nine cultural designs passed 63 content scenarios and nine isolated export jobs. All generated assets were inspected individually before integration. Ganesha was regenerated to simplify the composition and clarify the shorter tusk. The accepted figures and non-figurative artwork are documented in `cultural-review.md` and `artwork-manifest.json`.

Ik Onkar, Khanda, the Christian cross and the Wheel of Dharma use checked, embedded Noto glyphs. They are not AI drawings. Each compatible option and None was rendered; the optional जय भीम heading is real Devanagari text. No identity field selects or filters artwork.

Final visual review covered color and grayscale first pages, continuation pages, all mobile previews, no-photo layouts and Urdu. It found and corrected two header issues: the Christian cross needed a separate centered position without a portrait, and RTL arch positioning needed an explicit physical right reset plus a centered text measure. The corrected outputs were rendered again and visually inspected.

The large source illustrations initially produced two PDFs above 2 MB. Resizing the print variants to their actual physical placement retained approximately 450–600 dpi for small artwork and reduced all final scenario PDFs below 1.25 MB. The two failing export-size tests then passed.

Review files are under `output/template-collection/batch-3`, with optional-symbol proofs in `edge-checks`. These are digital print proofs. Physical printer output and review by members of each represented community have not been performed.
